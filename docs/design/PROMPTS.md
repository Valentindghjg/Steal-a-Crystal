Each plot has a low simple wooden fence, neat stone pedestals holding a few colorful crystals, and one gray training rock with an obvious yellow training circle outside its entrance. No HUD overlays, no marketing title, no unrelated buildings, no ornate fantasy castle, no forest that obscures layout. This is a proposed six-plot layout, not an assertion that all design decisions are final.
